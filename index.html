A LITTLE PLACE FOR REAL STORIES

Dear, Anonymous...

Tell me the story you never got to tell anyone. A first crush, a heartbreak, a friendship, a beautiful memory, an awkward moment — anything you want to get off your chest.

Send your letter 💌

No real names needed. Please keep yourself and others anonymous.

HOW IT WORKS

♡

1. Write

Put your story into words. It can be short, long, funny, messy or heartfelt.

✉

2. Send

Your submission is sent privately for review. It isn't posted automatically.

✦

3. Maybe featured

Selected stories may be turned into anonymous content, with identifying details left out.

YOUR LETTER

Dear, Anonymous... 💌

Write it like you're telling a trusted friend.

Please don't submit anything that could identify, embarrass, threaten, or put another person at risk. Every story should be reviewed before it is shared publicly.

Dear, Anonymous ♡
