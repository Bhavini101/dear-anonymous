To make the form actually work:

2. Create a new form and set your destination email to your email address.
3. The service will give you a form endpoint.
4. Open index.html.
5. Find:

6. Replace action="#" with the endpoint the service gives you.
7. Upload the three files (index.html, style.css, script.js) to your website host.
